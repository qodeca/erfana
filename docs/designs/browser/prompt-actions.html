Prompt actions

proposed not yet reviewed · design note README.md · spec 025 v0.12.0 · revised after review round 1

What answers the reviewer’s pick: the prompt-actions panel – a visible refinement control and four action buttons – and the instruction dialog, both anchored beside the outline over the still image, never inside the page, each showing where a send goes before the press; the send notice in the reserved pick row; and the messages for a failed capture, a failed write and a target with no agent. One static frame per state, drawn to scale with the system’s CSS. Build nothing from it until the owner approves it (025-FR-043).

How to read a frame. Every drawing is a picture (inert, aria-hidden); the caption names the surface and state, the spec ids, and – where the drawing rests on one of the owner’s answers (2026-11-01) – “Owner decision OD-n”. The labels Ask…, Change…, Prompt… and first-action focus with a banner are owner decision SPEC-6; the fresh-key-press rule is SPEC-7; full stops on spec-fixed sentences are OD-9. Frames are macOS unless tagged Windows. The page under the panel is the still image of the page (Layering card): nothing in it is interactive. Positions inside a frame are fixed per frame; in the app the component places the panel at the outline’s nearest side with room.
The deliberate choice. Both surfaces carry a 2px lime top edge, the pick frame’s colour (.erf-picker-owned, proposed on the Element picker card). Violet means “keys go to the page”, lime means “keys belong to your pick”; the panel and the dialog are where those keys go, so they wear the lime. It is identity, not the boundary – the floating surface’s border and shadow still draw the edge on any page. Lime never means “done”: the send notice’s glyph is neutral.
Modal inside the tab only. Both surfaces are role="dialog" with aria-modal="false": with the panel open the rows stay live at full strength – a toolbar click, ⌘L or F6 closes the panel without sending (025-FR-049); with the dialog open the rows are inert and only their buttons’ icons are dimmed (never the origin field, never a ring), while the terminal and the rest of Erfana stay reachable. The status slot always carries the chrome-side cue a page cannot draw: the panel or dialog variant of the banner with the Still image chip (Page paused in its place when frozen), or “Over a still image of the page – Esc or Cancel closes the dialog” once pick mode has ended.
Cards cited. Decided: Focus and keyboard, Menu (the Choose terminal and target menus), Form controls (multi-line field, error contract). Proposed: Dialog (anchored dialog, sheet), Buttons (secondary, ghost, icon, menu button, busy), Feedback (boxed banner, toast, hidden severity word), Badges, chips and keys, Element picker (including #room), Layering, Writing (ellipsis on Ask…, Change…, Prompt…; none on Explain).

Prompt-actions panel over the still image

An anchored dialog with a refinement control and a group of four buttons, not role="menu": Tab and Shift+Tab move and wrap, ↑ ↓ refine the element, Esc closes. Named “Actions for button.cta, 176 by 32”; the target and the key hint are its description.

Each action with keyboard focus

Explain focused is the first frame above. The panel alone, on the still image.

Instruction dialog over the still image

Opened by Ask…, Change… or Prompt…, replacing the panel and anchored to the same outline. Focus order: ← Actions, banner action (if any), field, the Sends to button, Cancel, Send, wrapping.

Send notice

In the pick row’s status slot – the reserved row – so it never moves the page or covers it. The actions are there at every width.

Capture-failed and shell-guard messages

Toasts are placed clear of the live view (over the terminal here); banners are boxed inside the panel or the dialog and rewritten the moment their cause changes.
