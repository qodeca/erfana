Picking

proposed not yet reviewed · design note README.md · spec 025 v0.12.0 · revised after review round 1

How the reviewer points at one element on a page Erfana does not own, and always knows where keys go: the lime pick frame in the page’s gutter (dashed while focus is elsewhere), the pressed Pick element toggle, the banner in the reserved pick row with one true clause per state – its panel and dialog variants are the chrome-side cue over the still image – the “Still image” chip (or “Page paused” in its place when frozen), and the two-tone outline and label that follow the mouse. One static frame per state, drawn to scale with the system’s CSS. Build nothing from it until the owner approves it (025-FR-043).

How to read a frame. Every drawing is a picture (inert, aria-hidden); the caption names the surface and state, the spec ids, and – where the drawing rests on one of the owner’s answers (2026-11-01) – “Owner decision OD-n”. Frames are macOS unless tagged Windows. The pages under the outline are third-party stand-ins in their own paint. Over the live page the outline is drawn by the engine (ADR 0007); on the still image Erfana draws the same look. The banner strings per state follow review round 1 (M-01); one true banner text per routing state is owner decision SPEC-2; full stops on spec-fixed sentences are OD-9.
Cards cited. Proposed with this design: Element picker (outline, label, frame and its away form, picked look, pick-owned edge, and the banner and narrow-room rules in #room), Origin field. Decided: Focus and keyboard (the one ring; the two-tone exception OD-2 asks to extend), Permission band (pressed signature, status slot), Menu. Proposed: Buttons, Badges, chips and keys, Tooltip, Layering, Highlights (not used: the outline is not a text highlight), Writing.

Pick-mode frame, banner and chips

The key-routing states made visible without colour alone: the frame (violet ring for Browse, solid lime for Pick, dashed lime for Out), the pressed toggle’s shape, one true banner clause per state, the chips, the crosshair. Forced colours: the pick frame turns dashed Highlight and the away frame dotted (drawn on the Element picker card).

Pick overlay and label

Element picker card look, on light, dark and accent-coloured pages, in every placement the note lists, and the ↑ ↓ refinement sequence with its visible end notes. The crosshair marks where the mouse is.
