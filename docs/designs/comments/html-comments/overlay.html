Comment overlay in the page

Everything on this card is drawn inside the previewed page, in a closed shadow root, by the preview preload. It is the only part of the comments feature Erfana cannot style with its own stylesheet – so this card shows where every colour comes from.

What is fixed by the plan, and what is still a value. The rules come from locked decisions and §10.2 / §10.5 of docs/designs/comments/html-preview-comments.md: the pin is a speech bubble at the element's top-right (decision 33); several threads on one element are one numbered pin; resolved threads hide their pin; pins are always visible, also outside comment mode, and can be hidden per tab (decision 20). Those are not re-opened here. Decided: the overlay shipped in v0.23.1 with the sizes and fills below, and changing one now means changing overlayStyles.ts, its theme and this screen together.

The source. src/preload/previewPageComments/overlayStyles.ts (the rules) and src/main/services/preview/comments/commentOverlayTheme.ts (the values). This card renders a mirror, overlay.css, with each value written back as its token.

The mapping: tokens resolved for the page

The page cannot see design-tokens.css, and must not: a var() in the page would read the page's custom properties, which the page controls. So main sends each value resolved, in commentState.theme, and commentOverlayTheme.test.ts pins each field to its token (R-012). The third column is baked from design-tokens.css by npm run design – nothing is typed.

Contrast, derived. The pin's fill against its ink stroke and content, the label's tag on its ink ground and the mode tab's text are all one pair: 8.39:1. Hover keeps the ink: 6.84:1. A damaged pin's ink ! on the warning fill: 8.00:1. The label's text: 17.62:1, and its size in the muted grey: 11.51:1. All clear the 4.5:1 small text needs.

Why the ring is two-tone. The accent alone on a white page is 2.09:1 – under the 3:1 a focus or selection indicator needs (SC 1.4.11). The 1px ink line on both sides of it is 17.62:1 there; on a black page the accent carries it instead. Whatever the page paints, one of the two edges clears 3:1. The pin's focus ring uses the same trick: an accent ring with an ink halo between it and the bubble.

Outline and label

Border box only, drawn instantly with no tween: a 2px accent ring with a 1px ink line on both sides. Selected – the element a menu or window is open for – adds a 12% accent fill. The label tag#id.class1.class2… 120×40 sits above the element's top-left, flips below it at the page top, and is clamped sideways. Tag in accent, the rest in paper, the size in grey, always dir="ltr" so a right-to-left page cannot reorder a selector. Two classes at most, then …, 40 characters at most, then a suffix.

The pin

A self-drawn square-cornered bubble, M2 2H22V17H7L2 22Z in a 24 × 24 hit box (WCAG 2.5.8). The tail tip sits on the element's top-right corner, 4px outward, so the bubble floats above the element rather than covering it. Near an edge it stays on the page: it is clamped to the page's visible area – never under its scrollbar – and keeps 4px inside it, so an element as wide as the page has its pin just inside the right edge. Only open threads are counted. One open thread shows two ink "text lines"; 2–9 the number in bold mono; ten or more 9+. A state is never colour alone: hover changes the fill and the cursor and adds a tooltip, selected inverts the whole bubble, damaged swaps the content for !. Pins are never animated.

Not drawn – and this is a state too: every thread resolved, the element not visible now (a hidden slide or tab), the element not found in the file, or pins hidden for this tab. Each of those threads is still reachable from the drawer, which is the guaranteed route to every comment.
A defect this card found on its first render, fixed. The number and the ! sat low, touching the bubble's bottom edge: pins.ts centred the text at y="13" with dominant-baseline: middle, but the bubble's body runs from 2 to 17 in the view box. They are now HTML text in a foreignObject over the body (PIN_BODY), centred by flex, and the text is CSS generated content so Find in page never counts it (E-022); the card draws them the same way.

Damaged and selected. A selected damaged pin keeps its warning fill under the selected accent stroke, so its ! stays ink – the selected accent on the warning fill would be 1.04:1, no step at all. Hover recolours an open pin only, never a selected or damaged one.

Comment mode

Three signals, never one: the toolbar toggle is pressed, a 2px accent frame runs inside the page edge, and a tab at the top centre says “Comment mode – Esc to leave”. The tab moves to the bottom while the pointer is within 48px of the top, so it never sits on what you are trying to pick. The cursor is the system crosshair. The band's height never changes.

The picture below is the page in comment mode, at rest: the frame, the tab at the top centre, and a shield over the refused frame. In the app, elements outline as the pointer passes and the tab jumps down near the top edge; the outline frames above draw that state. Frames that cannot be commented inside – a refused or srcdoc frame – get a transparent shield, so a click selects the <iframe> element itself (§19 R3-3).

Exceptions

Where the overlay departs from the system, with the reason. Each has a deviates: marker at its site in overlayStyles.ts – the stylesheet as a whole, the pin focus rule and the forced-colours block – and the same marker in the card's mirror, overlay.css.

WhereThe systemThe overlay, and why
Every colour and size Tokens only, as var(). Literals interpolated from commentState.theme. A var() inside the page would resolve against the page's own custom properties, which a hostile page sets. The literals come from the tokens through one table, pinned by a test (R-012).
Literals with no theme field Every value from the theme. The hairline, the spaces and the line height now come through the theme (hairline, space1, space2, space4, leading); the line height moved from a typed 1.4 to --leading-snug, 1.375. Still written as literals, each with no token of its own: the 12% selected and 24% flash fill mixes; the pin's SVG stroke widths 1.5 and 2 (selected), in view-box units; the label's 1ch gap before the size; the count's weight 700 (the value of --font-bold, which the theme does not carry); the geometry percentages (50% centring and clip, 100% SVG size). The page has no live region any more: the panel speaks the picker's sentences (E-022). The R-012 test lists exactly these.
Pin focus Focus card: the violet ring, and every control in the Tab order. The ring uses the passed-in accent with an ink halo, and pins are focusable but tabindex="-1": a page can carry hundreds, and the keyboard route to every thread is the drawer (§10.8). Recorded in the Focus card's Exceptions.
Forced colours System colours where the theme is replaced. Matches: outline, frame and flash take Highlight; label, tab and pin take Highlight / HighlightText. A selected pin inverts – Canvas body, Highlight stroke, line and count – so it still differs from an open one, and hover does not recolour a pin there. Listed so nobody “fixes” the literals in that block.
