Who is commenting

A comment carries its author's name – and email, if they give one – inside the HTML file. This card is where the reviewer is told that, once, in plain words, before anything is written.

Locked by the plan (decision 3, §3 “Privacy notice”, §7.9): name required, email optional; the notice is shown once to everyone, even when the name was already set in Settings – then the fields are pre-filled and the notice is the point. Identity lives in app-level settings only, never in a project, so a cloned repository cannot preset who you are. The step is step 1 inside the comment window, not a second dialog on top of it: nothing typed is lost and there is one focus trap, not two.

The notice is the whole truth, including the part that cannot be fixed: scripts in a previewed page can read the file they came from, so they can read the names in it (threat S6). Decided: this shipped in v0.23.1, and its copy is IDENTITY_COPY and SETTINGS_COPY in commentCopy.ts word for word.

The identity step

The step at rest. Continue with an empty name, or a malformed email, says what is wrong under the field (decided Form card: aria-invalid, aria-describedby, icon plus text). With a name, Continue turns the same window into the new comment, with your text field focused. Cancel closes the window; nothing is saved and the notice will show again next time.

Built from what exists. Buttons are the proposed Buttons card's .erf-button (the app still ships Dialog.css's .dialog-btn until button.css is adopted), the Settings notice is the proposed Feedback card's info banner with its visually hidden “Note:”. The fields, their errors and the checkbox follow the decided Form controls card, but that card has no shared stylesheet, so they stay drawn by this screen's comments.css mirror of CommentWindow.css – whether the decided card gains the comment window's multi-line field and a shared stylesheet is an open owner decision.

Erfana writes your name – and your email, if you add one – into the file next to each comment. Anyone who can open the file can read them: in Erfana, in the file's source, and in git history once the file is committed and pushed. In HTML files, scripts on previewed pages can read them too. Deleting a comment later does not remove it from git history.

The same two fields, saved on blur, affecting new comments only. The same warning sits here as a notice, so someone who sets their name in Settings first is still told. The section follows the shipping .settings-row layout with the Form card's controls – and one correction: descriptions are tertiary, not secondary. --color-text-secondary on the Settings surface measures 3.72:1, under the 4.5:1 that --text-sm needs; tertiary is 6.33:1. The shipping sections use secondary today; this card does not copy that.
