Comments drawer

The list of every comment on the page. It is the guaranteed keyboard route to every thread – pins are not in the Tab order, and a hidden, resolved or lost comment has no pin at all.

Where it lives. A flow sibling at the inline end of the page area – never absolute, so the native page shrinks beside it instead of being covered (Layering card: anything over a preview lives outside its rectangle). 320px wide (--comments-drawer-width), at least 280px; opens instantly, because the native view moves in one step and an eased width would race it. Under a 640px panel it takes the whole width, and “Show on page” closes it first. This is the shape the proposed Drawer pattern generalises from this drawer.

Built from what exists. The list follows the decided Rows and lists card – one Tab stop, ↑ ↓ Home End – with the Welcome list's one recorded extension: the active row's Open is a second Tab stop. The frames draw it with that card's row.css: .erf-row-list > .erf-row, the active row .erf-row--selected (a fill plus a 2px lime edge); comments.css adds only the two-line grid, the stretched button, the excerpt and the Open slot. Open and Try again are the proposed Buttons card's .erf-button--secondary .erf-button--sm; the empty, loading and error lines are the proposed States pattern's inline state; the note is the proposed Feedback card's info banner. The filter is a radio group in the selected signature, the checkbox is the decided Form controls card's. Text in the rows is --color-text-tertiary or brighter: 6.33:1 on the drawer.
Where the shipping drawer differs from the cards. v1 shipped in v0.23.1 with CommentDrawer.css; these frames draw the cards, and the strings are the shipped ones (DRAWER_COPY in commentCopy.ts).
  • Selected row. The frames mark the active row with .erf-row--selected, not aria-selected: the shipped list is a plain list of buttons, not a listbox, so aria-selected would be invalid there. The app ships .erf-comment-row--active, the same fill and edge, with a 12px start padding where the Rows card has 8px.
  • Empty, loading and error. The app draws each as one line of tertiary body text (.erf-comment-drawer__empty, .erf-comment-drawer__status), the error with a 14px inline icon. The States pattern gives the empty line a primary title (and the first-run hint its own tertiary line), loading its words-only progress form, and the error the error icon above the sentence.
  • Buttons. The app ships .erf-comment-action until button.css is adopted.
  • The note. The app fills it --color-brand-violet-muted with an --color-accent-primary edge (a deviates: marker in CommentWindow.css, recorded in the Feedback card's Exceptions) and says no severity word; the frames draw the Feedback card's info tone and its visually hidden “Note:”.
  • The filter. Still the drawer's own segments (.erf-comment-filter). The proposed Segmented control card is its planned replacement; it is not drawn here because the app has not adopted it.

The keyboard route

Tab reaches the close button, then the filter (one stop – ← → move the selection), the checkbox, then the list once. In the list, ↑ ↓ Home End move; Enter or a click is Show on page and focus stays in the list; Tab once more reaches that row's Open. On a not-found or damaged row the Enter action is Open in source instead, because there is nothing on the page to show.

The badge on the toolbar

The toolbar button carries the open count, plus · !1 when something needs attention. Its name says the same in words: “Comments, 3 open, 1 needs attention”. See the toolbar card.

Exceptions

WhereThe cardThe drawer, and why
Rows card – Tab stops Tab enters the list once. The active row's Open is a second Tab stop, as the Welcome list's Remove. Opening a thread then needs no shortcut to learn, and the cost does not grow with the list. Recorded in the Rows card's Exceptions.
Excerpt Rows card: nothing is ever cut. The first message is clamped to two lines. That rule is about an origin, where the cut part is the part that matters; an excerpt is a preview of text the window shows whole.
Width Sizes from tokens. The width is the --comments-drawer-width token; the 280px minimum is a literal, marked deviates: in CommentDrawer.css.
