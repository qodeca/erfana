Comments drawer

The list of every comment on the page. It is the guaranteed keyboard route to every thread – pins are not in the Tab order, and a hidden, resolved or lost comment has no pin at all.

Where it lives. A flow sibling at the inline end of the page area – never absolute, so the native page shrinks beside it instead of being covered (Layering card: anything over a preview lives outside its rectangle). 320px, at least 280; opens instantly, because the native view moves in one step and an eased width would race it. Under a 640px panel it takes the whole width, and “Show on page” closes it first.

Built from what exists. The list follows the decided Rows card – one Tab stop, ↑ ↓ Home End – with the Welcome list's one recorded extension: the active row's Open is a second Tab stop. The active row wears the Rows card's selected signature, a fill plus a 2px lime edge. The filter is a radio group in the same signature, the checkbox is the Form card's. Text in the rows is --color-text-tertiary or brighter: :1 on the drawer.

Live – keyboard only

Tab reaches the close button, then the filter (one stop – ← → move the selection), the checkbox, then the list once. In the list, ↑ ↓ Home End move; Enter or a click is Show on page and focus stays in the list; Tab once more reaches that row's Open. On a not-found or damaged row the Enter action is Open in source instead, because there is nothing on the page to show.

Rows card – Tab stops Tab enters the list once. The active row's Open is a second Tab stop, as the Welcome list's Remove. Opening a thread then needs no shortcut to learn, and the cost does not grow with the list. Recorded in the Rows card's Exceptions.
