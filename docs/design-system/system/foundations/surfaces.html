Surfaces and elevation

proposed reviewed 2026-08-31

Five surfaces, one order. A surface that sits in the layout is told apart by its fill; a surface that floats over it – a menu, a popover, a dialog – always carries a border and a shadow, because on a near-black app a shadow alone is invisible and a fill step alone is too small to see. Corners are square, scrollbars come from one utility, and nothing clickable is smaller than 24 × 24.

The stack

tertiary – sidebar, tree
secondary – panel
elevated – popover
dialog over the scrim

Bottom to top. The step column is the contrast between a surface and the one it sits on: it is not a pass mark (surfaces need no 4.5:1), it is how far apart two fills are. Under about 1.1:1 the eye stops separating them, and the edge has to come from a border.

Defect: elevated is not elevated. --color-bg-elevated resolves to the same colour as --color-bg-secondary – the step above is 1.00:1, no step at all – so a popover over a panel is separated only by its border and shadow. That is why the border-plus-shadow rule below is not optional. The fix is a colour decision (a fill one step lighter than the panel, as dark interfaces conventionally lift), recorded in the README defects.

The dialog is the darkest surface, not the lightest. It takes the app background and stands off the dimmed panel by the scrim, its border and --shadow-dialog. That is a decision, kept: the scrim does the lifting, and a dialog the colour of the app reads as "the app, focused".

Borders and corners

Do not mix the two models on one element. In Chromium, setting the standard scrollbar-width or scrollbar-color switches the ::-webkit-scrollbar rules off for that element, and the standard properties have no hover state. The utility uses the pseudo-elements (Electron is Chromium-only) and touches scrollbar-width only to hide. The live box above is that CSS; the region is focusable so a keyboard can scroll it.

Target size

Every pointer target is at least 24 × 24 CSS pixels – both dimensions (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8, AA). A 16px icon button meets it by padding its hit box to 24 × 24, not by drawing a bigger icon.

The spacing exception lets a target be smaller only if a 24px circle centred on it overlaps no other target and no other target's circle. In practice: two 16px targets need their centres at least 24px apart (a --space-4 gap). Also exempt: a link inside a sentence, a control the browser draws, and a target whose size is essential. A destructive action is never the smallest target on screen.

Declared, never read

Non-colour tokens nothing in src/ reads: --btn-height-lg, --icon-size-md, --icon-size-xs, --input-height-lg, --opacity-active, --shadow-focus, --shadow-none, --shadow-xl, --sidebar-min-width, --space-0, --z-base, --z-modal-backdrop. Each is a candidate for deletion or for the rule that was meant to use it.

Exceptions

WhatWhereWhy it is allowed
--header-height and --tab-height are odd (41px), off the 2px grid design-tokens.css; the panel headers and the editor tab strip The sidebar's panel header and the editor's tab strip sit side by side, so they share one height and one bottom edge. The odd pixel is inherited, and the reason for it is not recorded; changing it moves every header and tab at once. Keep it in the two tokens, read the tokens, and never derive another size from it. (EditorTab.css and ImageTab.css still type it as a literal – README defects.)

A shipping component that departs from this card carries a deviates: marker at the site and gets a row here, naming the file.
