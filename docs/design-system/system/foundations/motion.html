Erfana design system / Foundations / Motion

Motion

proposed reviewed 2026-08-31

Motion explains a change; it never decorates one. Durations come from the four --transition-* tokens, anything that loops or travels stops under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, and a busy state is announced in words, not only drawn. Transitions are already disciplined: of the 32 renderer stylesheets that declare one, 1 writes its duration as a literal. Animations are the problem.

Durations

Play – send every puck across its track at its own duration

Close it to send them back. Hovering a single track plays just that one. With reduced motion set, the pucks jump instead of travelling – this page obeys the rule it documents.

Easing

Every duration token bakes ease into its value, so any other curve has to be written by hand and can never be themed. Proposed: split the curve out of the duration, into three named curves. Each plot is progress (up) over time (right).

Two additions are proposed: the easing set above, and --duration-spin: 0.8s. Nothing in the scale today is longer than 0.3s ease, which is why every spinner in the app invented its own length.

One spinner – proposed, not shipped

This spinner does not exist in the app yet. Erfana has 6 spin keyframes, in 6 stylesheets – copies of a spin keyframe plus renamed clones – running at 3 different speeds. The proposal: one erf-spinner class and one keyframe in src/renderer/src/styles/utilities.css, sized from --icon-size-*, timed by --duration-spin, and nothing else redefining it.

A busy indicator is words first

The rule. The glyph is aria-hidden="true" – a spinning ring means nothing to a screen reader. The words go in a polite live region (role="status") that is already in the page before the text changes, or the change is not announced. The region being replaced carries aria-busy="true" while it loads and false when it is done, and the status ends with the outcome ("Project loaded", or the error), not with silence. Announce the start and the end, never each tick. 
<div class="project-tree" aria-busy="true">…</div>
<p role="status"><span class="erf-spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span> Loading project…</p>

When motion must stop

Anything that repeats forever, or travels, must stop under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. For some people looping or sweeping motion causes nausea and dizziness; the setting is how they tell you.

The rule lives once, in the entry stylesheet. index.css holds 1 app-wide reduce block, and it covers all 15 renderer stylesheets that animate. Under reduce, every keyframe animation runs once and ends at once: a spinner rests as a still ring, the camera flash (which ends at opacity 0) never shows, and a slide-in appears in place.

Why not per file. Per-file opt-in left stylesheets animating, and one file disabled the very same spin keyframe another left running – the predictable result of asking every author to remember. A component with a motion-safe replacement, such as the toast's opacity fade, sets it in its own reduce block with !important; a class beats the universal selector.

What the block does not cover: transitions. It ends animations. A hover that moves something by transform is a transition, and still moves under reduce – see the last row.
Kind of motionUnder reduce
Infinite spin, pulse or shimmerStop it. Keep the shape so the meaning survives.
Full-screen flash or wipeRemove entirely. Never negotiable.
Slide-in of a new elementNo travel: it appears in place, or cross-fades where the component opts in.
Colour or opacity change on hoverKeep. It does not move, so it is safe.
Transform on hover – a lift, a nudge, a scaleRemove the transform under reduce; keep the colour and shadow change, which carry the state alone. The entry block does not do this for you – the component's own reduce block must.
A one-time highlight (a comment pin after Save, “Show on page”)Nothing to change: it is a static box shown for a moment, not an animation. Proposed in the Comment overlay screen.
One thing that must never animate: the height of the HTML preview's permission band. The previewed page sits in a native view that moves in one step, so an eased height would race it and expose page pixels underneath the buttons. Instant height also means there is no reduced-motion case to handle.

Exceptions

None recorded. A shipping component that departs from this card carries a deviates: marker at the site and gets a row here, naming the file.

Defects this card names

  • Hover lifts keep moving under reduce. Dialog.css (primary and danger buttons), FileConflictNotification.css and ChatBubble.css move on hover with transform and have no reduce block; the entry block covers animations only.
  • No shared spinner. Every spinner is a local copy – see the count above.
