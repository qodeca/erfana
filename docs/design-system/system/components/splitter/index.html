Splitter

proposed not yet reviewed

A splitter is a 1px line in the layout with a 24px hit area over it. It is a focusable role="separator" that says how the space is shared (aria-valuenow), and the arrow keys move it. Hover, drag and focus all draw the same 2px lime line.

What ships today. The editor/preview divider (Editor/ResizableDivider.tsx) is an 8px grey bar that turns lime under the pointer. It has no role, no tab stop, no keyboard and no value – a keyboard or screen-reader user cannot resize the panes at all, and its 8px target is under the 24 × 24 floor. The README lists it under Open defects; this card is the shape to build against. The dock's sash is a second, separate splitter with a 4px hit area.

The keyboard contract

The WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern. The value is the share of the first pane, in percent, between 20 and 80 – the limits ResizableDivider.tsx already clamps a drag to.

A pointer that cannot drag. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7 asks for a way to do with one click what a drag does. The view-mode toggles (editor only, split, preview only) cover the extremes; for the split itself, the splitter's right-click menu offers Reset to 50% and the presets 30% and 70% (the Menu card).

Markup

<div class="erf-splitter" role="separator" tabindex="0"
     aria-orientation="vertical" aria-label="Resize editor and preview"
     aria-controls="editor-pane"
     aria-valuenow="62" aria-valuemin="20" aria-valuemax="80" aria-valuetext="Editor 62%"></div>

The panes on either side must not be positioned (or must sit in their own stacking context below the splitter): the hit area is a positioned ::before and wins over static siblings without a z-index, but a positioned pane later in the DOM would paint over its right half.

Open defects this card names

  • ResizableDivider (Editor/ResizableDivider.tsx, .css) – no role, no tab stop, no keyboard, no value, an 8px target, and z-index: var(--z-dropdown) to lift a divider that needs no lifting. Already in the README's Open defects.
  • Dock sash (AppDockLayout.css) – a 4px literal hit area, below the 24 × 24 floor.

Exceptions

None recorded. A shipping splitter that departs from this card carries a deviates: marker at the site and gets a row here, naming the file.
