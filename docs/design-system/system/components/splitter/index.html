Splitter

proposed not yet reviewed

A splitter is a 1px line in the layout with a 24px hit area over it. It is a focusable role="separator" that says how the space is shared (aria-valuenow), and the arrow keys move it. Hover, drag and focus all draw the same 2px lime line.

What ships today. The editor/preview divider (Editor/ResizableDivider.tsx) is a grey bar that turns lime under the pointer. It has no role, no tab stop, no keyboard and no value – a keyboard or screen-reader user cannot resize the panes at all, and its target is narrower than the 24 × 24 floor. The README lists it under Open defects; this card is the shape to build against. The dock's sash is a second, separate splitter, with a hit area that is also under the floor.

Every state

The keyboard contract

The WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern. The value is the share of the first pane, in percent. aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax are the clamp ResizableDivider.tsx already applies to a drag – read from there, not restated here.

One departure from the pattern, on purpose. The pattern's optional Enter collapses the first pane and restores it. Here Enter resets the split to 50% instead: the clamp means neither pane may collapse, and collapsing is already the view-mode toggles' job (editor only, preview only), so a second way to do it would only disagree with them. Reset is the one thing a keyboard user cannot otherwise do in a single key.

A pointer that cannot drag. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7 asks for a way to do with one click what a drag does. The view-mode toggles (editor only, split, preview only) cover the extremes; for the split itself, the splitter's right-click menu offers Reset to 50% and the presets 30% and 70% (the Menu card).

Markup

<div class="erf-splitter" role="separator" tabindex="0"
     aria-orientation="vertical" aria-label="Resize editor and preview"
     aria-controls="editor-pane"
     aria-valuenow="62" aria-valuemin="clamp floor" aria-valuemax="clamp ceiling"
     aria-valuetext="Editor 62%"></div>

The panes on either side must not be positioned (or must sit in their own stacking context below the splitter): the hit area is a positioned ::before and wins over static siblings without a z-index, but a positioned pane later in the DOM would paint over its right half.

Defects this card names

  • ResizableDivider (Editor/ResizableDivider.tsx, .css) – no role, no tab stop, no keyboard, no value, a target narrower than the 24 × 24 floor, and z-index: var(--z-dropdown) to lift a divider that needs no lifting. Already in the README's Open defects.
  • Dock sash (AppDockLayout.css) – a literal hit area, below the 24 × 24 floor.

Exceptions

None recorded. A shipping splitter that departs from this card carries a deviates: marker at the site and gets a row here, naming the file.
