Segmented control

proposed not yet reviewed

When the reader picks one of two to five choices and every choice should stay visible, use one .erf-segmented: a role="radiogroup" of role="radio" cells with one tab stop. The checked cell gets a fill and a 2px lime bottom edge – the Rows and toggle signature, never colour alone. Cells are equal width; they share one row from --erf-segmented-break (280px by default) and below it each cell takes a full row. The group has a name – a visible label or aria-label.

What this card does not decide. Six or more choices, or choices that need a sentence each, are a select or a menu (the Form controls and Menu cards). An on/off switch is a checkbox or a toggle button (Buttons). Radios inside a toolbar – the Markdown view modes – stay under the Toolbar card's one-tab-stop contract, drawn with .erf-button--toggle; this card is for a group that stands on its own in a panel or a band. The count is the Badge card's .erf-counter.

Every state

Drawn at the app's scale on the drawer's surface (--color-bg-secondary). The reference instance is the comment drawer's “Show” filter (CommentDrawer.tsx, the filter group; CommentDrawer.css, the .erf-comment-filter rules).

Width: equal cells, one row or a stack

Equal width says “these are peers”; a ragged row says one choice matters more. So a cell's basis is 0 while the group is at least --erf-segmented-break wide – every cell takes an equal share of the row – and very large below it, so every cell wraps to its own full-width row. No media query, no script. The default break is 280px (calc(var(--space-20) * 7)), which holds three cells of up to about eleven characters each. An instance with longer labels raises the break to three times its widest cell; the comment filters, with “Needs attention” and a count, set it to 432px (calc(var(--space-24) * 9)). A label never truncates – a truncated choice is a choice the reader cannot read.

Contrast

Text needs 4.5:1; the lime edge and the ring are non-text and need 3:1. Disabled text is exempt and is measured only for the record.

The keyboard contract

One tab stop for the whole group (roving tabindex: the checked cell is 0, the rest -1). Moving checks – the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern – because applying a filter or a view is cheap. When a choice is costly to apply, use a select with an explicit Apply instead.

Open defects this card names

  • No Home or End. The shipping filter's key handler covers the four arrows only.
  • A ragged wrap. The shipping options are content-sized and wrap one at a time, so in the 320px drawer “Needs attention” drops to a second row on its own and the three look unequal.
  • The count is a bare figure. .erf-comment-filter__count is a mono number with no shape, where every other count in the app is an .erf-counter.

Exceptions

None recorded. A shipping segmented control that departs from this card carries a deviates: marker at the site and gets a row here, naming the file.
