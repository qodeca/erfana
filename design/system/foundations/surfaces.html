Surfaces and sizing

Depth, edges and the standard dimensions of a control.

Corners are square. Always. --border-radius is 0 and it is not a variable to override. The single exception is --border-radius-circle for things that are genuinely round, like an avatar or a status dot.

Elevation

Control heights

A pointer target must be at least 24 × 24 CSS pixels — WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8. --btn-height-sm sits exactly on that line, so anything smaller fails. A destructive or irreversible action should never be the smallest target on screen.
