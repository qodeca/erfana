Erfana design

16 cards, generated from the folder itself. Every page reads a synced copy of the app's design-tokens.css and the real bundled Cascadia Mono, so no value here is transcribed by hand — and npm run design -- --check fails in CI when the copy and the app disagree.

Check a card's status before citing it. decided is binding; proposed is a direction of travel, not settled law.

New here? Read README.md first. It carries the rules this page cannot show: what decided binds you to, when a card may be promoted from proposed, the six conditions a card must meet to be done, how to record a deviation, and the list of defects these cards have decided but the app still ships.
Rebuild with npm run design after adding a card or changing a token. This index, tokens.css and the fonts are generated — do not edit them.
