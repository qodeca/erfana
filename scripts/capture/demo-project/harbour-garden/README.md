# Harbour Street Community Garden

Welcome to the handbook of the Harbour Street Community Garden: twelve shared plots, a
greenhouse and a compost corner, looked after by volunteers from the neighbourhood.

This folder is the garden's shared notebook. The committee keeps it up to date; anyone who
helps out can suggest a change.

## Plots

| Plot | Looked after by | Growing this season |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Mira and Tomas | Beans, courgettes |
| A2 | The Thursday group | Potatoes |
| B1 | Ines | Herbs and edible flowers |
| B2 | School garden club | Sunflowers, pumpkins |
| C1 | Shared | Soft fruit |
| C2 | Free – ask the committee | – |
