# Planting calendar

When each crop goes in, and when to expect a harvest. Dates are for our sheltered spot near the
harbour; inland gardens usually start two weeks later.

| Crop | Sow | Plant out | Harvest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad beans | February (greenhouse) | March | June |
| Potatoes | – | April | July to August |
| Runner beans | April (greenhouse) | May | July to September |
| Courgettes | April (greenhouse) | late May | July to September |
| Sunflowers | April | May | August |
| Pumpkins | May (greenhouse) | June | October |
| Garlic | – | October | next July |

Ask the committee before you add a new crop, so the rotation stays on track.
