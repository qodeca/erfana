# Getting involved

Anyone who lives nearby can help. You do not need any experience – the regulars are happy to
show you around.

## How to join

1. Come along to a Saturday work morning (9:00 to 12:00).
2. Put your first name on the volunteer board by the shed.
3. Pick a job from the list below, or ask what needs doing.

## What to bring

- Gloves and sturdy shoes
- A water bottle
- A small bag if you want to take produce home

Tools, seeds and tea are provided.

## Good to know

- Check the [planting calendar](planting-calendar.md) before you sow anything.
- Kitchen scraps go in the compost bins, never in the plots; the
  [compost guide](compost-guide.md) says what can go in.
- Please close the gate behind you.
